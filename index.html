Get clear on the project before building.

Avoid vague requirements, repeated explanations, and agents making conflicting assumptions. Project-Kickoff asks focused questions, saves your decisions, and turns the answers into a practical plan.

STEP 01

Understand the project

Clarify the problem, who it is for, and what success looks like. For an existing project, inspect the code and identify gaps.

STEP 02

Agree on a plan

Choose what to build first, how it should work, and the technical approach. You approve the important decisions.

STEP 03

Prepare the workspace

Set up project instructions, a task tracker, supporting tools, and starter files within the scope you approve.

STEP 04

Get ready to build

Check that tasks have clear outcomes and that the plan is ready. Pass the saved context to Agent-Team when you choose to begin.

How they fit together

Project-Kickoff gets the project understood and ready. Agent-Team carries out the plan and checks the results. You decide when building begins.

What gets recorded

  • Your decisions and the reasons behind them.
  • The scope you approved and questions still open.
  • Actionable tasks with a clear definition of done.
  • Project documents describing the requirements, design, and implementation plan.

What remains yours

  • Choose the tools, task tracker, scope, and design direction.
  • Approve decisions and any proposed installations before setup.
  • Keep existing work and decide what should change.
  • Resume later without reconstructing the interview.

Give Agent-Team the plan, without starting over.

Project-Kickoff saves the approved plan, task references, and project decisions in a handoff file. Agent-Team uses that context to coordinate developers and reviewers, fix problems, and bring checked changes together.
