# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Product owners and developers defining a new software project, auditing an existing codebase, or planning a major revision before implementation.

## Product Purpose

Project Kickoff turns incomplete product intent into approved product, experience, technical, and execution records plus an optional bounded Agent-Team handoff.

## Positioning

A resumable, approval-driven planning workflow that asks one material question at a time and keeps planning separate from runtime implementation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Current Project Kickoff release: 0.5.0.
- Five approval stages lead through four lifecycle states.
- The 0.5.0 handoff checker was released against Agent-Team 7.3.1.
- Native Agent-Team 8.0.10 can consume approved Project Kickoff facts during setup while preserving producer and compatibility provenance.
- Project Kickoff does not start Agent-Team or implement product features as a side effect.

## Brand Commitments

The Project Kickoff name, creator attribution to thebpandey, version truth, and thoughtful decision-led voice must remain visible.

## Evidence on Hand

The skill metadata, changelog, handoff checker, templates, tests, and current repository documentation.

## Product Principles

- Ask only what materially changes the plan.
- Record decisions and approvals durably.
- Keep one live tracker and explicit ownership boundaries.
- Preserve historical compatibility truth instead of rewriting provenance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public site must remain keyboard accessible, responsive, readable with reduced motion, and understandable without decorative imagery.
